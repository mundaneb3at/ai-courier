# F7 - Google Drive folder missing, paused or not syncing

**Symptom:** popup `courier inbox: folder missing (is Google Drive running?)`, SEND says `Your Drive send
folder was not found`, or the host says a message the person sent never reached them.

**Checks**
1. Click the Google Drive icon by the clock: is it paused, signed out, or showing errors?
2. `doctor.ps1`: the `inbox =` line says `ok` or `missing`.
3. In File Explorer, do `G:\My Drive\from-host` and `G:\My Drive\from-<name>` both exist?

**Fix:** resume or sign in to Google Drive for desktop and wait a few minutes. A SEND made while Drive was
paused uploads once Drive runs again. If the Drive letter changed (not G), tell the host: `settings.json`
needs the new letter.

**Still broken:** make a ticket with route F7. If Drive is fine and the host still gets nothing, it is their
side: card F8.
