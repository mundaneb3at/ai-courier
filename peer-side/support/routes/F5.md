# F5 - Forgot a passphrase

**Symptom:** the person forgot their own passphrase (the words typed at SEND), or the host changed theirs.

**Checks**
1. Whose passphrase: theirs (used at SEND) or the host's (saved once with `-SetKey host`)?

**Fix:** theirs: pick a new one (4+ random words) and tell the host **by phone or in person**; they re-save
it on the host side. The host's: they say the new one by phone, and the person repeats INSTALL step 7. Never type
a passphrase to the AI, in chat, email or a file. The AI must never ask for it.

**Still broken:** make a ticket with route F5 (never put a passphrase in it).
