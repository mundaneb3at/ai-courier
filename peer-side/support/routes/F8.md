# F8 - the host's side cannot download (the host side)

**Symptom:** The host says the host side cannot download the person's messages ("pull failing", their Google
token expired).

**Checks**
1. Card F7 first: is the person's own Google Drive running and their `from-<name>` folder still shared
   with the courier address?

**Fix:** nothing else to do on this side. The host reconnects their courier account (their INSTALL, "When a
push needs you"). Messages already sent are not lost: they wait in the Drive folder.

**Still broken:** The host handles it; no ticket needed unless they ask for one (route F8).
